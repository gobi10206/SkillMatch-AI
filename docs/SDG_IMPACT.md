# SDG Impact - SkillMatch AI

## Distinguishing projected vs measured impact

Everything below is projected impact - what the platform is designed
to measure and move, based on its feature set. It is not a claim of
measured, real-world impact; no such data exists until the platform
has real users and real deployment history. Any future report using
this platform's data should carry the same distinction explicitly.

## SDG 4 - Quality Education

What the platform measures: learning paths created, learning modules
completed (LearningProgress status/completion_pct), skill improvement
over time (UserSkill verification over time), certifications logged.

How the product design supports it: the informal-experience extractor
(Resume Analyzer) surfaces existing competency that formal credentials
might miss, and the learning path breaks unfamiliar goals ("Learn SQL")
into micro-learning steps rather than one intimidating block.

## SDG 8 - Decent Work and Economic Growth

What the platform measures: job/internship matches generated,
applications tracked through the Kanban pipeline, career readiness
score trends.

Explicit boundary: the platform does not and cannot measure actual
employment outcomes (whether someone was hired) without external
verification data it doesn't currently integrate - this is called out
in the AI safety rules against guaranteeing employment.

## SDG 10 - Reduced Inequalities

What the platform measures: PWA/low-bandwidth usage patterns,
geographic reach of registered users, fairness dashboard metrics
(selection-rate parity, aggregated and consent-based).

How the product design supports it: the informal/freelance/volunteer
experience path is a deliberate design choice to recognize skill
gained outside formal employment or education - the exact population
traditional job portals underserve. Low-bandwidth PWA support extends
reach to users on limited connectivity or low-end devices.

## What this platform does not claim

It does not claim to have closed any employment or education gap. It
claims to provide the instrumentation - explainable skill extraction,
transparent scoring, fairness-aware matching, and measurable learning
progress - that would let a real deployment demonstrate impact against
these SDGs with real data, which is the honest scope for a project at
this stage.
